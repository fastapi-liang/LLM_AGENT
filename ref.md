without prior hearing of the accused(§ 33 para. 4 of the German Code of Criminal Procedure (StPO)), upon application by the Public Prosecutor's Office of Magdeburg dated 29 May 2026, and pursuant to §§ 111e para. 1, 4, 111j para. 1 StPO in conjunction with §§ 73 para. 1, 73a, 73c, 73d, 76a of the German Criminal Code(StGB), a property seizure order(asset arrest) is hereby issued in the amount of EUR 162,000.00(= 192,312.406238 USDC) against the movable and immovable assets of the accused:

被告：李世民，生于2001年9月24日，中国身份证号码 430523200109244331，OKX账户号码 236507493881257984 的持有人。
Li Shimin, born on 24.09.2001, holder of Chinese ID Card 430523200109244331,, account holder of the OKX account number 236507493881257984.

债务人若存入与扣押金额等额的款项，即可阻止扣押的执行，并请求解除扣押执行（根据《刑事诉讼法》第111e条第4款第2句）。
Upon depositing an amount equal to the seizure amount, the debtor may prevent enforcement of the seizure and request that the enforcement be lifted(§ 111e para. 4 sentence 2 StPO).

**理由**
**Reasons**

根据迄今为止进行的调查，未知犯罪分子作为所谓在线交易平台 https://xautcrypto.com 的运营者，
Based on the investigations conducted thus far, unknown perpetrators acting as operators of the purported online trading platform https://xautcrypto.com

通过故意虚假的利润承诺，诱使受害方 XXX 在2025年6月4日至2025年7月18日期间，从其 Crypto.com 账户向地址 0x115eb0667951be38acc2e20bc0e510b2bd655c4 转移了总计 162,000.00欧元（等同于192,312.406238 USDC）。
induced the injured party, XXX, through deliberately false promises of profit, to transfer a total of EUR 162,000.00(= 192,312.406238 USDC) from his Crypto.com account to the address 0x115eb0667951be38acc2e20bc0e510b2bd655c4 during the period from 4 June 2025 to 18 July 2025.

受害者在私人接触/关系/交易骗局（即“杀猪盘”）中损失了约 162,000.00欧元。
The victim lost approximately EUR 162,000.00 in the context of a private contact/relationship/trading scheme.

Rathenow eG 人民银行提交的银行对账单及受害者本人的陈述证实了这一点。
This is evidenced by the submitted bank statements from Volksbank Rathenow eG and the victim's own statements.

受害者在转移这些资金时，相信自己正与一个真实的人建立真实的私人关系，并基于该关系建立的信任，通过交易所服务提供商 Crypto.com 将资金发送至目标地址，这些地址被认为是与涉嫌犯罪结构相关的洗钱服务的一部分。
The victim transferred these funds under the belief that he was engaged in a genuine private relationship with a real person and, based on the trust developed from that relationship, sent the funds via the exchange service provider Crypto.com to destination addresses believed to be part of a money-laundering service connected to the suspected criminal structures.

这些转账产生的交易数据已被知晓，并且可以类似于 IBAN 系统中的银行账户数据进行追踪。
Transaction data resulting from these transfers became known and can be traced in a manner similar to bank account data in the IBAN system.

受害者在交易所 Crypto.com 拥有一个账户。
The victim maintained an account with the exchange Crypto.com.

他从该账户在以太坊区块链上进行了10笔交易，总计 192,312.406238 USDC。
From this account, he conducted 10 transactions on the Ethereum blockchain, totaling 192,312.406238 USDC.

随后，通过多次区块链转账对这笔资金进行了追踪。
This amount was subsequently traced through various blockchain transfers.

调查人员确定，这些资金已作为借贷安排的一种形式存入了所谓的 Aave 协议。
Investigators determined that the funds had been deposited into the so-called Aave Protocol as a form of lending arrangement.

在此过程中，资产的价值保持不变。
The value of the assets remained unchanged during this process.

基于其他调查（例如柏林州刑事警察局）的发现，这种资金存入行为显得既符合逻辑又必要，因为如此庞大的金额无法被迅速提取。
Such depositing of funds appears, based on findings from other investigations(for example by the State Criminal Police Office of Berlin), both logical and necessary because amounts of this magnitude cannot be quickly withdrawn.

通过中间地址 0x8ab8ab15b59389f69e6e38a8b71aa8d9ec7312b0，单笔资金随后被转移至 0xa78e8b7592318f2a0b29a03fdd778addef4f889。
Via the intermediate address 0x8ab8ab15b59389f69e6e38a8b71aa8d9ec7312b0, individual amounts were then transferred to 0xa78e8b7592318f2a0b29a03fdd778addef4f889.

从地址 0xa78e8b7592318f2a0b29a03fdd778addef4f889 开始，通过各种服务进行了多次货币兑换。
Starting from address 0xa78e8b7592318f2a0b29a03fdd778addef4f889, multiple currency exchanges were carried out through various services.

USDC金额（与美元挂钩的稳定币）被转换为USDT（由Tether Limited发行的与美元挂钩的加密货币），然后再次转换回来。
USDC amounts(stablecoins pegged to the US dollar) were converted into USDT(a US dollar-pegged cryptocurrency issued by Tether Limited) and then converted back again.

最终，区块链分析使调查人员能够将从受害者处非法获取的资产追踪至加密货币交易所 OKX 的一个账户。
Ultimately, blockchain analysis enabled investigators to trace the assets unlawfully obtained from the victim to an account at the cryptocurrency exchange OKX.

根据 OKX 提供的信息，在其他交易中，被告 李世民（生于2001年9月24日，中国身份证号码 430523200109244331）向 Aave 协议存入了 200,033.518086 美元，并在随后将其提取。
According to information provided by OKX, among other transactions, USD 200,033.518086 was deposited into and later withdrawn from the Aave Protocol by the accused Li Shimin, born on 24 September 2001, holder of Chinese ID Card 430523200109244331.

该行为构成了根据德国《刑法典》第261条第1款第1句（洗钱罪）以及第263条第1款、第3款第2句第2项（加重诈骗罪）应受处罚的犯罪。
The conduct constitutes offenses punishable under §§ 261 para. 1 sentence 1(money laundering) and 263 para. 1, para. 3 sentence 2 no. 2 StGB(aggravated fraud).

对犯罪行为的怀疑基于萨克森-安哈特州刑事警察局的调查结果、受害者 xxx 的陈述以及 OKX Europe Ltd 提供的信息。
The suspicion of criminal conduct is based on the findings of the State Criminal Police Office of Saxony-Anhalt, the statements of the victim xxx, and information provided by OKX Europe Ltd.

由于对被告的怀疑已得到证实，有理由假设他通过归咎于他的犯罪行为，获得了《刑法典》第73条第1款和第73d条意义上的 162,000.00欧元的金钱利益。
Due to the substantiated suspicion against the accused, there are grounds to assume that, through the offense attributed to him, he obtained a pecuniary benefit in the amount of EUR 162,000.00 within the meaning of §§ 73 para. 1 and 73d StGB.

根据《刑事诉讼法》第111e条第1款，财产扣押令对于确保没收等值资产是必要的。
The property seizure order is necessary to secure confiscation of equivalent value pursuant to § 111e para. 1 StPO.

发布财产扣押令需要对犯罪行为的简单怀疑，以及假设在最终判决中将下令没收替代价值（纽伦堡-菲尔特地区法院，BeckRS 2021, 18138；Köhler 收录于 Schmitt/Köhler, StPO, 2025年第68版，§ 111e, 第4段）。
The issuance of a property seizure order requires a simple suspicion of a criminal offense and the assumption that confiscation of substitute value will be ordered in the final judgment (Regional Court of Nuremberg-Fürth, BeckRS 2021, 18138; Köhler in Schmitt/Köhler, StPO, 68th edition 2025, § 111e, para. 4).

没收请求和保全需求都必须存在具体的迹象，尽管不需要正式的证明（Hellerbrand, wistra 2003, 202）。
Concrete indications must exist for both the confiscation claim and the need for security, although formal proof is not required(Hellerbrand, wistra 2003, 202).

如果该措施实际上剥夺了受影响者对其几乎所有资产的控制权，则需要进行特别仔细的审查和论证（联邦宪法法院，JR 2015, 540；NStZ 2006, 639）。
Particularly careful examination and justification are required where the measure effectively deprives the affected person of control over nearly all of their assets(Federal Constitutional Court, JR 2015, 540; NStZ 2006, 639).

平衡考量还必须考虑到国家没收犯罪污染资产的利益。
The balancing exercise must also take into account the state's interest in confiscating criminally tainted assets.

此外，关于该措施的持续时间，必须遵守宪法的比例原则。
Furthermore, the constitutional principle of proportionality must be observed with regard to the duration of the measure.

对照这些标准，本案满足了法律要求。
Measured against these standards, the legal requirements are met in this case.

没有能够达到相同效果的替代措施可用。
No alternative measures capable of achieving the same effect are available.

此外，被告可以通过存入扣押金额来解除扣押。
Furthermore, the accused may obtain lifting of the seizure by depositing the seizure amount.

被告据称居住在中华人民共和国这一事实并不妨碍该措施的执行。
The fact that the accused is allegedly residing in the People's Republic of China does not preclude the measure.

在本案中，国家保全资产的利益具有特别的分量。
In this case, the state's interest in securing the assets carries particular weight.

必须假设可能会有人采取措施阻挠国家对所获价值的没收要求（《刑法典》第73、73c、76a条）。
It must be assumed that efforts may be undertaken to frustrate the state's claim for confiscation of the value obtained(§§ 73, 73c, 76a StGB).

在此背景下，财产扣押令不仅是必要的，而且总体上也是成比例的。
Against this background, the property seizure order is not only necessary but also proportionate overall.

省略了被告的事先听证，因为这会危及调查的目的（《刑事诉讼法》第33条第4款第1句）。
A prior hearing of the accused is omitted because it would jeopardize the purpose of the investigation(§ 33 para. 4 sentence 1 StPO).

**法律救济途径通知**
**Notice of Legal Remedies**

本命令可通过提出抗诉（Beschwerde）的方式提出质疑。
This order may be challenged by way of complaint(Beschwerde).

抗诉必须以德语书面提出，或由发布命令的法院书记处进行记录。
The complaint must be filed in writing in the German language or recorded by the court registry of the court that issued the order.

如果受影响者未获自由（被羁押），抗诉也可由受影响者被官方命令羁押的机构所在地区的地方法院书记处进行记录。
If the affected person is not at liberty, the complaint may also be recorded by the registry of the local court in whose district the institution is located where the affected person is being held by official order.

法院可以在不进行口头听证的情况下对抗诉作出裁决。
The court may decide on the complaint without conducting an oral hearing.
